Clear Prop: Part 107 Trivia

Privacy Policy

Honey Badger Laboratories LLC  ·  Effective November 20, 2026  ·  Applies to iOS app

⚠  Disclaimer: Clear Prop is an unofficial study tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA. Always verify questions and answers against current FAA regulations before your knowledge test.

Short version: Clear Prop does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device. We never see it.

Who We Are

Clear Prop: Part 107 Trivia is published by Honey Badger Laboratories LLC, based in North Carolina. You can reach us at support@honeybadgerlabs.io.

Data We Collect

We collect no data. Clear Prop is designed from the ground up to operate entirely on your device, with no analytics SDKs, no account system, no advertising network, and no server of our own that the app talks to. Our Apple App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any identifier
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or crash reports routed to our servers
  • Any content you create in the app

Progress Stored on Your Device

Your study progress — question history, scores, settings, and the name you enter (optional) — is saved locally on your device only, in the app's own storage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

To keep the 5-minute free trial fair, the app also saves how many seconds of the trial you have used in your device's secure iOS Keychain. This is a single number with nothing personal in it, and it stays on your device.

If you save a backup from the app, the backup file goes only where you choose to put it (for example, Files or AirDrop). We never receive it.

Purchases

Clear Prop offers a free 5-minute trial and an optional one-time in-app purchase for Full Access. Both are handled as App Store in-app purchases. All purchases are processed exclusively through Apple's StoreKit using Apple's standard payment infrastructure. We never see, handle, or store your payment information. For purchase-related questions, see Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Online Multiplayer (Game Center)

Clear Prop supports turn-based online matches between two players using Apple Game Center. When you use this feature, Apple manages player matching and game state. The only information exchanged is what Apple's Game Center handles — your Game Center alias and match data — governed by Apple's own privacy practices. We do not operate a game server or store match data ourselves.

Game Center participation is entirely optional. You can study and take the Mock Exam offline without ever using it, and you can sign out of Game Center at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Apple Platform Services

As an iOS app distributed through the App Store, Clear Prop is subject to Apple's standard platform data practices, which may include crash reporting and analytics that Apple collects independent of us. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us aggregated crash and usage reports that do not identify you. We do not receive identifiable data from Apple's platform services.

If You Contact Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you and to fix the problem you reported. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone. Ask us and we will delete your emails.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

Because your progress lives only on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • Erase progress: in the app, go to HQ and tap Erase progress. This deletes your history, scores and settings from the device.
  • Delete the app: deleting Clear Prop removes its stored progress. We have no copy.
  • Trial record: the trial-time number in the iOS Keychain can remain after the app is deleted, so a reinstall does not restart the trial. It contains no personal information.
  • Purchases and Game Center: these records are held by Apple. You can manage them in your Apple Account and Game Center settings.
  • Emails to us: we keep support emails only as long as needed to help you, and we delete them on request.

Children

Clear Prop does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13. The app contains no features that require or encourage sharing personal information.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our data practices, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top. Because the app currently collects no data, any change significant enough to affect you would require a new app update that you could review before accepting.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@honeybadgerlabs.io. We read every message.
