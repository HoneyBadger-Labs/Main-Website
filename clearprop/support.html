Common Questions

The app crashed or won't open — what do I do?

Start with the basics: force-close the app and reopen it. If that doesn't help, try restarting your iPhone.

If the app still won't open, delete it and reinstall from the App Store. Your progress is stored locally on your device. If you've made an export backup (see below), you can restore it after reinstalling. If you haven't made a backup, reinstalling will start fresh.

Still broken? Email us with your iOS version and iPhone model and we'll dig in.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Clear Prop keeps all progress on-device only — there's no cloud sync. To transfer to a new phone, use the Export and Import tools built into the app:

  1. On your old phone, open the app and go to HQ (the settings/home hub).
  2. Tap Export Progress. The app creates a backup file and shows the system share sheet.
  3. AirDrop the file to your new phone, or save it to Files and move it over.
  4. On your new phone, open Clear Prop, go to HQ, and tap Import Progress. Select your backup file.

The import replaces all existing progress on the new device in one transaction — either all of it lands or none of it does, so you won't end up with a half-mixed state.

I think a question or answer is wrong. How do I report it?

We take question accuracy seriously — this is a study tool for a real FAA exam. If something looks off, please email us at support@honeybadgerlabs.io with the question text and a note on what you think is incorrect. Include a source (FAA ACS, AIM, regulations, etc.) if you have one handy.

Clear Prop includes both original questions and the FAA's own published sample questions. We'll check every report against current source material and update the question bank in the next release.

I want a refund. How do I get one?

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Clear Prop purchase, and select "Request a Refund."

We don't handle payment information ourselves, so we can't process refunds directly — Apple is the right path.

What is the FAA Part 107 knowledge test?

The FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate is required to fly a drone commercially in the United States. To earn it, you must pass a 60-question knowledge test at an FAA-approved testing center, scoring 70% or higher.

Clear Prop helps you study through a spin-the-wheel trivia format covering airspace, weather, regulations, emergency procedures, and other topics on the exam. It also includes the FAA's own published sample questions.

The app is a study aid only. For official guidance on the Part 107 certification process, visit faa.gov/uas/commercial_operators.

I purchased Full Access but the app still shows the trial. Help?

This can happen when a purchase doesn't fully process. Try these steps in order:

  1. Go to HQ in the app and tap Restore Purchases. This re-syncs your purchase record from Apple.
  2. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy Full Access.
  3. Force-close the app and reopen it after restoring.

If restoring doesn't fix it, email us at support@honeybadgerlabs.io and we'll sort it out.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. All 650+ questions are built into the app. Study mode, practice exams, and local two-player matches all work offline with no internet connection required.

The only feature that needs a connection is online turn-based matches via Apple Game Center.
