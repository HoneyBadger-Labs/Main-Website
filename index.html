How we build

Our approach

Small team. No overhead. Ship things that work.

One problem at a time

Every venture starts with a real gap — something we've personally run into or watched others struggle with. We don't build solutions looking for problems.

Works offline first

If the core function requires an internet connection, we've built it wrong. Resilience is a feature, not an afterthought.

Lean by design

One founder, no overhead, no investors to answer to. That keeps us fast and keeps the product honest — built for users, not for a pitch deck.

Ship early, iterate honestly

We put things in front of real users early, say what's not ready, and improve based on what actually matters — not what looks good in a demo.

Leadership

One founder.

Jonathan

Jonathan Sherlin started Honey Badger Laboratories to take ideas far enough to find out whether they work. His background in IT, project management, and data analytics taught him how the pieces of a complex system fit together. He uses AI the way a builder uses any good tool — to move faster and take on work that used to require a whole team.

The lab builds practical tools for people who like to be prepared and do things themselves. For Jonathan, the interesting part starts when there's something people can actually try. That's when he finds out which assumptions hold up, what needs to change, and which ideas are worth turning into businesses of their own.

The name comes from Badger, his first retired racing greyhound.
